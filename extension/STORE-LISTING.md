Full description:

```
Download images and videos directly from the Meta Ads Library — then keep watching the advertisers that matter.

Meta Library Downloader now does more than a single save. Open any Facebook Ad Library search and you can download clean media, mark winners, open the offer, export the real ad copy, and get a notification when a watched page launches something new.

Key features
• Download any ad's image or video in the best available quality. Videos save as a clean MP4 whenever Meta exposes one — not a broken .m3u8 file.
• Spot winning ads instantly. Any ad running 30+ days gets a crown and a golden outline.
• Bulk download an entire page in one pass. Progress shows in real time: "⬇ 6/24 downloaded."
• Spy on a page to see an advertiser's playbook: longest-running creative, average runtime, image vs video split, and how many 30+ day winners they have.
• Open the landing page behind any ad in one click.
• Export to CSV: the actual primary text, headline, CTA, destination URL, Library ID, and days running. Extension buttons and "Open Drop-down" are stripped out. Opens correctly in Excel.
• Watch an advertiser: paste a Page ID or Ad Library link in the popup (or tap Watch on an ad). Chrome notifies you when that page publishes a new ad.

Perfect for media buyers, DTC founders, agency researchers, and creative strategists who live in the Meta Ads Library.

Works on the Facebook Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library) in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, and other Chromium desktop browsers.

Privacy
The extension reads the Ad Library page to find ads and media URLs. Your watch list and recent downloads stay on your computer. Checking a watched advertiser uses your logged-in Facebook session to open that advertiser's Ad Library page. No account with us is required.
```
