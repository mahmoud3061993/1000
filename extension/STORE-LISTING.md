Full description:

```
Download images and videos directly from the Meta Ads Library — then build a swipe file without leaving the page.

Meta Library Downloader now does more than a single save. Open any Facebook Ad Library search and you can download clean media, mark winners, and export the copy in a few clicks.

Key features
• Download any ad's image or video in the best available quality. Videos save as a clean MP4 whenever Meta exposes one — not a broken .m3u8 file.
• Spot winning ads instantly. Any ad running 30+ days gets a crown and a golden outline.
• Bulk download an entire page in one pass. Progress shows in real time: "⬇ 6/24 downloaded."
• Spy on a page to see an advertiser's playbook: longest-running creative, average runtime, image vs video split, and how many 30+ day winners they have.
• Export to CSV: advertiser name, ad copy, Library ID, media URL, and days running.
• Annotate every ad with a note. Notes stay on your computer and show up the next time you open the page.
• Color-label your swipe file (winner, tested, later, angle, offer). Five colors, one click each.
• Recent downloads in the popup — the last 5 ads you saved, with advertiser name and time.

Perfect for media buyers, DTC founders, agency researchers, and creative strategists who live in the Meta Ads Library.

Works on the Facebook Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library) in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, and other Chromium desktop browsers.

Privacy
Everything runs locally on your device. The extension reads the Ad Library page to find ads and media URLs. Notes, labels, and your recent download list never leave your computer. No account required.
```
